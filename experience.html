September 17 & 18 · Durham, NC

The Experience

Duke Cinematic Arts and echo will co-present a two-day public experience built around I LOVE BOOSTERS, the new feature from filmmaker, musician and organizer Boots Riley, on September 17 and 18 in Durham. Both days are free, both are open beyond the university, and Riley will be present for all of it.

Thursday · Format 01
Sept 17

Community Screenings

The program opens Thursday, September 17, with two community screenings at the Rubenstein Arts Center Film Theater on Duke's campus — a 1:30 PM screening followed by a faculty panel, and a 7:00 PM screening followed by an in-person Q&A with Riley.

1:30 PM · Screening + faculty panel
7:00 PM · Screening + Q&A with Boots Riley

Admission is free and open to the public, with no Duke affiliation required.

Brunch with Boots Riley

It continues Friday, September 18, with Brunch with Boots Riley, a special edition of echo's monthly Unstuck Workshops at echo's Hunt Street hub, opened to all ages for this edition.

Presented in collaboration with Duke Cinematic Arts, this will be a discussion among local artists, filmmakers, young creatives, students and community voices about what it takes to build a creative life that is both meaningful and sustainable.

About the brunch
Friday's conversation

Three threads

The conversation is built around three threads:

  1. The film itself
  2. Creative controlRiley's process for holding creative control while working inside mainstream distribution.
  3. Building the TriangleWhat the Triangle would need to build in order to grow its own creative entrepreneurs and give them somewhere to scale.
Brought to you by

Seven organizations, one vision

These events are brought to you by Duke Cinematic Arts, echo, the Mary Duke Biddle Trent Semans Foundation, Screen/Society, the Experimental Film Lab at the John Hope Franklin Humanities Institute, Duke's Department of Art, Art History & Visual Studies, and NEON.

Duke Cinematic Arts echo Mary Duke Biddle Trent Semans Foundation Screen/Society Experimental Film Lab · John Hope Franklin Humanities Institute Duke Art, Art History & Visual Studies NEON

Individually, these organizations fund films, teach them, screen them, distribute them, and pay young people to make them. Together, they are working towards a unified vision of increasing the support available to entrepreneurs and creative businesses in this community, and building the infrastructure that lets them stay and scale here. Two days with Boots Riley is one visible expression of that work, which will continue to scale in the coming years.

Foundation support has facilitated I LOVE BOOSTERS to play in Durham at no cost.
