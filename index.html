Format 02 · echo Unstuck Workshops · Special Edition

Brunch with Boots Riley

FRI · SEPT 189:00 AM – 12:00 PM EST · 404 Hunt St, Suite 500All ages welcome
Special edition

A special edition of echo's Unstuck Workshops, open to all ages. Pull up a chair for an intimate brunch conversation with filmmaker, musician, and cultural provocateur Boots Riley.

No keynote. No polished presentation. Just a candid conversation with local artists, filmmakers, young creatives, and community voices about what it takes to build a creative life that is both meaningful and sustainable.

Artists have to have something they're more passionate about than the art itself.

Boots Riley · RogerEbert.com

What's an Unstuck Workshop?

echo's monthly hands-on session for getting past the thing you're stuck on.

Small rooms, real talk, & something to show for it by the time you leave. This September the series brings national creative voices to Durham — opening this edition to everyone, at every age.
