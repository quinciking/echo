Step inside the I LOVE BOOSTERS Universe

Two days of free screenings and a community conversation, built around I LOVE BOOSTERS, the new feature from filmmaker, musician and organizer Boots Riley. Free public screenings at the Rubenstein Arts Center through Cinematic Arts’ Screen/Society, plus a special community conversation hosted in partnership with echo at their community hub in Durham.

Made possible by a generous grant to Duke Cinematic Arts from the Mary Duke Biddle Trent Semans Foundation, supporting collaborations between Duke and Durham. Duke co-sponsors include Duke Cinematic Arts, the Experimental Film Lab at the John Hope Franklin Humanities Institute, and Duke’s Department of Art, Art History & Visual Studies.

Format 02 · echo Unstuck Workshops · Special Edition

Brunch with Boots Riley

FRI · SEPT 18echo’s community hub in DurhamInvitation-onlyFeaturing DPS, Duke, NCCU & moreAll ages
Special edition

Presented in partnership with echo and hosted at echo’s community hub in Durham, Brunch with Boots Riley is a special, all-ages edition of echo’s monthly Unstuck Workshops.

The conversation brings together local artists, filmmakers, young creatives, students and community voices to discuss what it takes to build a creative life that is both meaningful and sustainable. It is built around three threads: the film itself; Riley’s process for holding creative control while working inside mainstream distribution; and how the Triangle can grow its own creative entrepreneurial ecosystem.

This event is invitation-only, featuring partner community organizations like DPS, Duke, NCCU and more.

What's an Unstuck Workshop?

echo's monthly hands-on session for getting past the thing you're stuck on.

Small rooms, real talk, & something to show for it by the time you leave. This September the series brings national creative voices to Durham — opening this edition, by invitation, to creatives of every age.

At the brunch

Durham makers at the table

EspréssateFounder, Iasia Montana

Espréssate is a Cuban-inspired mobile espresso bar based in Durham, founded in 2022 by Iasia Montana. The cart brings a professional barista and Cuban-inspired coffee menu to weddings, corporate events and private parties across the Triangle, using beans roasted locally by Little Waves Coffee Roasters.

Dos Avocados MediaFounder, Dan Smith

Dos Avocados Media is a Durham-based, artist-run documentary storytelling and production company founded by Dan Smith. He specializes in documentary-style video, photography, and story consulting for small businesses and communities across North Carolina.

NOSHFounder, Wendy Woods

NOSH is a Durham restaurant serving eclectic, southern-inspired breakfast and lunch near Duke University, founded by Durham native Wendy Woods. Known for its brunch favorites and community presence, NOSH also offers catering for events across the city.

Sugar SymphonyFounder, Ariana H.

Sugar Symphony is a small creative bakery founded by Ariana H., specializing in custom cakes, cupcakes, cookies and seasonal desserts. Every treat is made with care, bringing people together to make any occasion a little sweeter.
